## Recommended key changes

1. **cbs-2024-vance-cbp-one-mic-cut#1: change Misleading → False.** PolitiFact says the literal description is wrong: it is "a scheduling tool, not an application for asylum or parole", and app users aren't "illegal" migrants. The `mapping_note` claim that "the literal mechanism is roughly right" contradicts the source. Also trim `text` to the sentence PolitiFact rated, or note that the "open border wand ... facilitation of illegal immigration" tail is unrated.
2. **trump-joint-2025-inflation-eggs-energy#2 (keyed False): mark it as accepting False or Misleading,** or change it to Misleading. The 48-vs-41-years point is the key's own arithmetic. PolitiFact says "it's been about four decades" and faults only the hedged "perhaps ... in history" clause.
3. **trump-joint-2025-inflation-eggs-energy#0 (keyed Misleading): mark it as accepting Misleading or False.** FactCheck.org's evidence ("Economic growth was strong, and inflation had fallen significantly") contradicts "economic catastrophe" rather than merely adding context.
4. **abc-2024-harris-afghanistan-camp-david#2 (keyed False): mark it as accepting False or Misleading.** PolitiFact's headline calls the remark "misleading" and the DoD confirmed no troops are fighting in war zones. The literal "combat zone" wording is still contradicted.
5. **biden-sotu-2024-economy#8 (keyed Misleading): mark it as accepting Misleading or False,** and make it consistent with the Vance superlative claim (keyed False). Both claims make a literal superlative that the checker contradicts. Either key both by the superlative (False) or note why a half-true compound claim ("9% to 3%" is correct) earns Misleading.
6. **biden-sotu-2024-economy#7:** already notes that Misleading is defensible. Encode that as an accepted alternative label so the harness doesn't score it as a miss.
7. **Rules note (no label change):** for Half True claims where the number itself is wrong (Biden "$15" insulin, Johnson "17%"), the firm Half True→Misleading rule overrides the definition of Misleading ("literal words accurate or nearly so"). Either add a README line saying the firm PolitiFact mapping wins over the definitions, or downgrade those two claims to `judgment`.
8. **Source-grounding notes (no label change):** `trump-joint-2025-social-security-ages#0` (no checker ruled on "over $500 billion") and `biden-sotu-2024-economy#6` (a single unexplained sentence) rest mostly on the key's own reasoning. Consider adding `judgment` caveats or dropping them from the `firm`-style headline score.

A simple way to apply items 2 to 6 is an `accepted_labels` array per claim, scored as exact-match when the system's label is in the array.
